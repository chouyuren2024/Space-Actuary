| Orbit | Low Earth Orbit (LEO) | Orbits Earth once every ~95 minutes. | Orbit | Geostationary Orbit (GEO) | Orbital period matches Earth's 24-hour rotation, appearing fixed over one spot in the sky to watch an entire hemisphere with continuous ground contact.
